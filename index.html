98 Main Road, Makaraka

The corner shop at the crossroads

A butcher has stood at the Makaraka crossroads for over a century, right where State Highway 35 sets off around the East Cape. The sign out front still says it best: no bum steers here.

You get meat cut in front of you, a straight answer on what to cook and how long to cook it, and change from what the supermarket charges for less.

  • Cut to order, not pre-packed
  • Local corner butchery, Gisborne through and through
  • Honest advice on cuts, quantities and cooking
